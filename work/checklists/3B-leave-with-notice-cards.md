# 3B Leave with notice: packing cards

Draft v0.2 (content only, not print layout), 16/11/2026. Scenario B: leaving by car with about 1–2 hours' notice (flood, wildfire, told to evacuate).

## Change log

- **v0.2 (16/11/2026).**
  - Sweden now cited as **SE26** (June 2026 evacuation list, SE26 p.19) instead of the 2018 edition. SE26 adds support for waterproof outerwear and spare clothes, a **map and compass**, hearing aids, and food and water "to last a few days".
  - Every "(gap research – verification pending)" label replaced with the v0.2 status (GAP §n ✅ / ✏️ / ❓, official source named). **Corrections:** "sturdy leads" and "bowls" are now credited to the **Blue Cross** leaflet, not the RSPCA; RSPCA's flood-line hours are 8am–6pm; flood-water depths now use the **Environment Agency** figures (about 15 cm can knock a person over, 30 cm of flowing water can float a car, about 60 cm can carry away a vehicle).
  - **Quantities changed:** the home medicines buffer referred to on Card 2 is now "7 days' extra, working towards a month" (was 7 days); Card 1 cash now refers to the household's per-adult amount (DECISION 16).
  - New items: compass; act early on a flood warning; ring the council about rest-centre pet policy; last-resort advice if dogs must be left; call 999 if in immediate danger; no warnings for surface-water flooding.
- **v0.1 (16/11/2026).** First content draft.

> **Cards are assigned to people in the household plan.** Each card is one job. Whoever holds a card does that job and nothing else until it is done, then helps with "Before we drive off". If there are fewer people than cards, one person takes more than one card.

**Why cards and not a source list.** No UK source gives a grab bag list (matrix, topic 23). These cards are built from the take-with-you lists in DE (p.21, p.39), FR (p1), NO (p.18) and SE26 (p.19), BRC's flooding list (091838), the UK utility and evacuation advice (UK-GOV 091213, 091230; UK-GOV-PLAN p.2, p.7) and, where marked GAP, `work/research/gap-research.md` v0.2. DE's basic rule: one rucksack per person, "do not take more for each family member than fits into a rucksack", so both hands are free (DE p.21). Keep packed items "somewhere easy to reach" (FR p1). DE: "you cannot be just starting to pack your emergency pack" when you have to leave (DE p.8), so as much as possible should be packed in advance. SE26 frames its list as one to prepare in advance: "Make a list of essential belongings if you are required to quickly evacuate" (SE26 p.19).

**Markers** (as in `3A-stay-put-supplies.md`): **[1 source]** = one publisher only; **(not in sources – check)** = added from general knowledge; **GAP §n ✅** = gap research v0.2 section n, verified on the named official page; **GAP §n ✏️** = verified after v0.2 corrected it; **GAP §n ❓** = still unverified.
