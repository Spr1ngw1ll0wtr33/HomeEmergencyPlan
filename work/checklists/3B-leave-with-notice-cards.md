# 3B Leave with notice: packing cards

Draft v0.1 (content only, not print layout), 16/11/2026. Scenario B: leaving by car with about 1–2 hours' notice (flood, wildfire, told to evacuate).

> **Cards are assigned to people in the household plan.** Each card is one job. Whoever holds a card does that job and nothing else until it is done, then helps with "Before we drive off". If there are fewer people than cards, one person takes more than one card.

**Why cards and not a source list.** No UK source gives a grab bag list (matrix, topic 23). These cards are built from the take-with-you lists in DE (p.21, p.39), FR (p1), NO (p.18) and SE (p.9 (pr. 16)), BRC's flooding list (091838), and the UK utility and evacuation advice (UK-GOV 091213, 091230; UK-GOV-PLAN p.2, p.7). DE's basic rule: one rucksack per person, "do not take more for each family member than fits into a rucksack", so both hands are free (DE p.21). Keep packed items "somewhere easy to reach" (FR p1). DE: "you cannot be just starting to pack your emergency pack" when you have to leave (DE p.8), so as much as possible should be packed in advance.

**Markers** (as in `3A-stay-put-supplies.md`): **[1 source]** = one publisher only; **(not in sources – check)** = added from general knowledge; **(gap research – verification pending)** = relies on `work/research/gap-research.md`.
