## How the references work

- Source IDs (UK-GOV, BRC, DK, NO, SE, FI, DE, FR and so on) are those in `work/evidence-matrix.md`. Page or screenshot references are as cited in the extracts in `work/evidence/`.
- **[1 source]** = only one publisher supports the item (the UK-GOV family counts as one, as does the Ready Scotland family).
- **(not in sources – check)** = added from general knowledge; not supported by any source.
- **(gap research – verification pending)** = relies on `work/research/gap-research.md`, which is being checked against the official pages. Re-check these items when that revision is finished.
- **SUGGESTION** = a figure or choice proposed in the matrix or this draft, not a source claim.
- **DECISION n** = refers to item n in the matrix list "Conflicts needing a household decision".
- Agreed decisions applied: 3-day core plus 7-day-plus column; water at 3 L per person per day for drinking and cooking (the source range of 2–3 L is shown); stock log to become a Calc spreadsheet.

## Open questions for the household

1. **Who is in the household?** Number of people (P) and dogs (D), so the formulas can be filled in. Any special diets, babies, medical equipment, mobility or hearing needs?
2. **How many days?** The 3-day core is fixed. How soon to build to 7 days, and should the store go further (DE says 10 days) given the home may be cut off by flooding?
3. **How much cash?** No source gives an amount. Set a figure for the 3-day core and for 7 days.
4. **Water for washing and flushing:** how much to store, beyond the 3 L per person per day for drinking and cooking? No source gives a figure.
5. **Water for the dogs:** ask the vet for a daily amount per dog.
6. **Stored water:** how often to change water in your own containers ("several months", DK, or "for years", NO)?
7. **Cooking:** no-cook food only, or a camping or spirit stove used outdoors? If a stove, how much fuel? Is there a sheltered outdoor space?
8. **Candles:** keep them as a backup with the safety rules, or torches only?
9. **Heating:** blankets and one warm room only, or an indoor-rated heater or wood stove? Who would we stay with if the house got too cold, and would they take the dogs?
10. **Generator or 230 V battery unit:** yes or no? If yes, how to run it safely (no UK source covers this).
11. **Carbon monoxide alarm:** fit one? Not in any source, but the gap research points to it.
12. **Radio:** FM, DAB or both? Which stations and frequencies?
13. **Medicines:** is a 7-day extra supply possible for everyone (and for the dogs)? Who orders repeats, and when?
14. **Leaving:** pre-packed bag per person, or pack from the cards on the day? How much water goes in the car (6 L per person, FR, or a bottle each, DE)?
15. **Documents:** copies only, or some originals? Keep duplicates with someone outside the home?
16. **Where do we go?** Main destination, alternative destination, and somewhere that will take the dogs (rest centres may not). Which routes, and which roads flood?
17. **Meeting points:** one close to home and one further away?
18. **Who has which card?** Assign the eight 3B cards, and who checks the store.
19. **Review:** every 6 months (UK-GOV) or once a year (FR)? Is a 2-month "nearing expiry" window right?
20. **Priority Services Register:** is anyone eligible (UK-GOV 091206)?
21. **Leaving the house:** lock up, leave a note, tell someone where we have gone? (No source covers these.)
22. **Fridge and freezer when leaving:** leave on, or empty and switch off?

## Not supported by the sources

**Added from general knowledge (not in sources – check):**
- Carbon monoxide alarm (no source mentions CO alarms).
- Drinking-water amount for dogs (sources say to include pets but give no amount).
- Counts per person for torches, blankets, power banks, hand sanitiser, wet wipes, gloves, buckets and rubbish bags; numbers of spare battery sets; a spare tin opener; a spare lead. These are SUGGESTIONS.
- Thermos flask for keeping boiled water hot at home (DE lists a thermos only for the emergency pack).
- Dog medication buffer of 3 / 7 days (mirroring the human buffer).
- The 3-day food quantities (DE's 10-day table × 0.3; the 7-day × 0.7 figures are the matrix's).
- On the 3B cards: counting everyone and every dog into the car; checking every card's bags are in the car; telling a named contact where you are going; leaving a note; closing and locking windows and doors; what to do with the fridge and freezer.
- The 2-month "nearing expiry" window and the other highlighting rules in the stock log.

**Relying on the gap research (verification pending):**
- Washing and hygiene water reference figure (10 L per person per day, GOV.UK Prepare as reported).
- Fridge and freezer hold-over times (Food Standards Agency, as reported).
- Full freezer stays cold longer.
- Battery backup for digital landlines.
- Floodline number for England (the core sources give 0345 988 1188 only for Scotland).
- Generator safety (outdoors only, away from doors and windows, no backfeeding through a socket, CO alarms).
- Dogs: bowls; vaccination and microchip records; sturdy leads; rest centres may not take pets; pet buddy or kennels; never risk your life for an animal; keep dogs indoors away from rising water.
- Alternative routes and checking flood warnings for the route.

**Gaps no source covers** (noted in the checklists, not filled): water treatment in UK sources; power bank size; keeping medicines cool in a winter power cut; how long bagged toilet waste can be kept; UK guidance on heaters, stoves and generators; rest centres; registering where you have gone; iodine tablets (not included here; UK arrangements depend on location, see the matrix, topic 13).
