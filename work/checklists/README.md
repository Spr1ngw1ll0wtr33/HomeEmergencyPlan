## How the references work

- Source IDs (UK-GOV, BRC, DK, NO, SE26, FI, DE, FR and so on) are those in `work/evidence-matrix.md` (v0.2). Page or screenshot references are as cited in the extracts in `work/evidence/`. "SE" (2018 edition) appears only in notes saying what the old edition said.
- **[1 source]** = only one publisher supports the item (the UK-GOV family counts as one, as does the Ready Scotland family).
- **(not in sources – check)** = added from general knowledge; not supported by any source or by the gap research.
- **GAP §n** = relies on `work/research/gap-research.md` v0.2, section n, with the official source named in brackets. **✅** = verified on that page on 16/11/2026; **✏️** = verified, but v0.2 corrected what v0.1 said; **❓** = still unverified (page blocked or not found), not relied on.
- **SUGGESTION** = a figure or choice proposed in the matrix or this draft, not a source claim.
- **DECISION n** = refers to item n in the matrix list "Conflicts needing a household decision" (16 items in v0.2; numbers 1–15 unchanged from v0.1, 16 is new: how much cash).
- Agreed decisions applied: 3-day core plus 7-day-plus column; water at 3 L per person per day for drinking and cooking (the source range of 2–3 L is shown); stock log to become a Calc spreadsheet.

## Open questions for the household

1. **Who is in the household?** Number of people (P), adults (A) and dogs (D), so the formulas can be filled in. Any special diets, babies, medical equipment, mobility or hearing needs?
2. **How many days?** The 3-day core is fixed. How soon to build to 7 days (now backed by NO and SE26), and should the store go further (DE says 10 days) given the home may be cut off by flooding?
3. **How much cash per adult?** (DECISION 16.) SE26 is the only source with a figure: "Around 1,000 SEK in cash at home per adult" (not converted here). Set a pound amount per adult for the 3-day core and for 7 days, and agree to spend and replace it now and then (SE26: "use cash occasionally").
4. **Water for washing and flushing:** how much to store, beyond the 3 L per person per day for drinking and cooking? No source gives a figure; the 10 L per person per day figure is no longer on GOV.UK.
5. **Water for the dogs:** ask the vet for a daily amount per dog. GOV.UK says only that more water is needed for pets.
6. **Stored water:** check taste and smell at each 6-monthly check (meets SE26's "once or twice a year")? And how often to replace it regardless ("several months", DK, or "for years", NO)?
7. **Cooking:** no-cook food only, or a camping stove used outdoors (FSA: "most are designed for outdoor use only")? If a stove, how much fuel? Is there a sheltered outdoor space?
8. **Candles:** keep them as a backup with the safety rules, or torches only?
9. **Heating:** blankets and one warm room only, or an indoor-rated heater or wood stove? Who would we stay with if the house got too cold, and would they take the dogs?
10. **Generator or 230 V battery unit:** yes or no? If yes, how to run it safely (HSENI advice is in the 3A Safety box; no GB page was found; ask an electrician about a changeover switch).
11. **Carbon monoxide alarm:** fit a battery-powered one? Now supported by HSENI (Northern Ireland regulator), though no core source mentions it.
12. **Smoke and heat alarms:** is there at least one smoke alarm on every floor and a heat alarm in the kitchen (NFCC), and are they interlinked? Add alarms in bedrooms and the living room?
13. **Radio:** FM, DAB or both? Which stations and frequencies?
14. **Medicines:** is 7 days' extra possible for everyone (and for the dogs)? Could the prescriber allow a month's supply (SE26)? Who orders repeats, and when?
15. **Leaving:** pre-packed bag per person, or pack from the cards on the day? How much water goes in the car (6 L per person, FR; a bottle each, DE; "a few days", SE26)?
16. **Documents:** copies only, or some originals? Keep duplicates with someone outside the home? Back up digital copies (SE26)?
17. **Where do we go?** Main destination, alternative destination, and somewhere that will take the dogs (rest centres may not: ring the council about its pet policy). Which routes, and which roads flood?
18. **Meeting points:** one close to home and one further away?
19. **Who has which card?** Assign the eight 3B cards, and who checks the store.
20. **Review:** every 6 months (UK-GOV) or once a year (FR)? Is a 2-month "nearing expiry" window right?
21. **Priority Services Register:** is anyone eligible (UK-GOV 091206; Ofgem's list, GAP §4)? Register with the electricity network operator, the supplier and the water company separately (Essex & Suffolk Water delivers bottled water to PSR customers who cannot collect if the water is off for more than six hours).
22. **Digital landline:** is the home phone already digital? If anyone relies on it, ask the provider for the free battery backup (GAP §4).
23. **Leaving the house:** lock up, leave a note, tell someone where we have gone? (No source covers these.)
24. **Fridge and freezer when leaving:** leave on, or empty and switch off?
