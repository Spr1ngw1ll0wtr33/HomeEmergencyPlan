# Family plan tool: specification (agreed 23/11/2026)

## Purpose
A tool for the two of us to record our own decisions, information and preparations for each section of the general guidance (`guidance/combined-guidance-v2.md`). It produces our family plan and a to-do list.

## Where it runs and privacy
- One self-contained file, opened in Firefox on either PC (Zorin or Linux Mint Cinnamon). It works offline and needs no account.
- Everything entered stays on the PC. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing personal goes on GitHub.
- Usually used on one PC, with the two of us sitting together. The other PC can open the same saved file.
- Passwords are never recorded. The tool asks only whether we know them and where any reminder is kept.

## Sections
- One section for each heading in the guidance v2: Getting ready, Scenario A, Scenario B, Scenario C, and Keeping things up to date.
- Any section can be marked **"not relevant to us"**. It is then left out of the plan and the to-do list.

## Questions in each section
The questions are tailored to the section's content and follow this general pattern:
1. **Our plan:** what we intend to do. Free text, plus specific prompts. For example, "Know your risks" asks what we think our risks are and which scenarios are most likely.
2. **Resources, tools and equipment:** for each item the guidance recommends (for example an FM radio, or a planned escape route), ask "Do we have …?" with the choices **Yes / In progress / No / Not sure**, plus an optional note.
3. **Where it is stored.**
4. **How we check it works**, and how often.

For example, Documents asks:
- do we have hard copies of each document, are they in a waterproof pouch, and where are they kept;
- do we have cloud copies, where are they stored, how do we access them, and do we know the password.

## Saving and progress
- **Saves as we go** on the PC.
- **Save to file:** downloads a plan file we can keep as a backup.
- **Open a saved file:** loads a part-finished or older plan so we can carry on and update it over time.
- **Visual tracker:** shows, for each section, whether it is not started, part done, complete or not relevant. It also shows overall progress.

## Outputs (downloaded as `.odt` files for LibreOffice Writer)
1. **Family plan.** For each relevant section:
   - an introduction explaining what the section covers;
   - the general guidance and why it matters, taken from the guidance v2;
   - **our response**: our decisions, information and plans.
2. **To-do list (a separate document).** Every "Do we have …?" item, colour-coded:
   - **green** = Yes;
   - **amber** = In progress;
   - **red** = No or Not sure.

   Each item is shown with its section and any note. We work through the list, update the tool, and download a fresh list.

## Parked for later
Checklists and the food log, which will come from the specific plan.
